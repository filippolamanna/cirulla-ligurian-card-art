# Cirulla — edited Chkobba court cards and back

These 13 images are **modified versions** of cards from the *Chkobba* deck
drawn by **Abjiklam**, published on Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Chkobba) under the
**Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International** licence
(CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

They are used as the court cards and card back of the "Ligurian" deck in the
iPhone game *Cirulla* by Filippo Lamanna. As the licence requires, these
modified versions are released under the same licence, **CC BY-SA 4.0**.
You may share and adapt them, also commercially, as long as you credit
Abjiklam (original artwork) and Filippo Lamanna (modifications), link the
licence, and release your own adaptations under CC BY-SA 4.0 too.

## What was changed

- The card's own white body and black outline were removed (the game draws
  its own cream, rounded card underneath), and the canvas was cropped to the
  card body (`viewBox="2 2 85 127"`).
- A non-standard `<def>` element was renamed to `<defs>`.
- File names: `trèfle` written as `trefle`.
- No colour or line inside the artwork was changed.

`svg/` holds the edited vector files; `png/` holds renders at 6× (510×762 px,
transparent background) for convenience.
